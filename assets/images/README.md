# Photography

Empty until the shoot happens. The site renders captioned grey placeholders in the
meantime, so it stays presentable.

Eight images needed. The seven mosaic shots, in the order they appear on the page:

1. `kitchen-wide.jpg` — the kitchen mid-service, window light. Landscape, this is

2. `pastry-hands.jpg` — close on hands lattice-cutting pastry. Landscape.
3. `pate-sliced.jpg` — the finished pâté en croûte, sliced. Portrait, tall block.
4. `table-laid.jpg` — the table dressed, glasses poured. Landscape.
5. `teaching.jpg` — Dominique explaining to two guests. Landscape.
6. `souffle.jpg` — the soufflé coming out of the oven. Landscape.
7. `market.jpg` — the market, early morning. Landscape.

Plus:

8. `dominique.jpg` — vertical portrait of Dominique in the kitchen, 4:5.

Notes for the shoot:
- Natural light. The palette is warm and muted; hard flash will fight it.
- Hands and process matter more than plated beauty shots. The product being sold is
  the day, not the dish.
- Guests should look like the target audience, and should be recognisably enjoying
  themselves without performing.
- Get written permission from anyone identifiable before publishing.

Export at roughly 2000px on the long edge, JPEG quality 80, which keeps each file
under about 400KB. Nothing here needs to be larger.
